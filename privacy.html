VaultGenie Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy outlines how VaultGenie ("the Application"), developed by Rareș-Mihail Cristea, safeguards your information on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

1. Absolute Zero Data Collection

VaultGenie was built upon a non-negotiable principle: your cryptographic keys, passwords, and finances belong solely to you:

  • Zero Telemetry: We do not track when you open the app, what items you store, or which websites you visit.
  • Zero Server Storage: Your vault data is encrypted locally using Argon2id and AES-256-GCM. We operate zero cloud databases that store your credentials.
  • No Analytics SDKs: There are no third-party tracking libraries, analytics trackers, or commercial advertising frameworks in the binary.

2. Face ID, Touch ID & Biometrics

Biometric authentication is handled exclusively by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric templates remain encrypted inside Apple's hardware Secure Enclave. VaultGenie never has access to raw biometric data.

3. Credential AutoFill & Passkeys

The AutoFill Credential Provider and Safari Extension operate within isolated iOS app extensions. Credentials are decrypted only when you authenticate with biometrics and select an item to insert.

4. Receipt Scanner & Financial AI

OCR recognition of receipts and AI subscription detection are computed locally on your device using Apple's Vision and CoreML frameworks. Images and receipts are never transmitted to external AI cloud APIs.

5. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Subscriptions and Lifetime Pass payments are processed directly by Apple. We never collect or store your credit card or billing credentials.

6. Contact Developer

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, contact: support@vaultgenie.app.
