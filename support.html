🛡️ Security & Zero-Knowledge Protocol

VaultGenie employs an authentic Zero-Knowledge architecture:

  • Your master password is never transmitted across the network or stored on any server.
  • Keys are derived locally using Argon2id and encryption uses AES-256-GCM.
  • Face ID and Touch ID biometric authentication use Apple's hardware Secure Enclave.
  • If you lose your master password without an emergency recovery kit, your vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the 14-day Free Trial work?

All users receive an unrestricted 14-day free trial on first launch. You have complete access to password vaults, passkeys, TOTP generator, AI subscription auditing, and receipt scanning without artificial paywalls.

How do I enable 1-Tap AutoFill on iOS?

Go to your iPhone/iPad Settings → Passwords → Password Options, turn on "AutoFill Passwords and Passkeys", and check VaultGenie.

Is VaultGenie compatible with Bitwarden / Vaultwarden?

Yes. VaultGenie natively imports and exports Bitwarden JSON encrypted or unencrypted format, and can sync directly with self-hosted Vaultwarden server instances.

How does the DeskStacks synergy work?

If you also use DeskStacks on macOS, DeskStacks can display a secure Vault Capsule widget on your desktop powered by VaultGenie's shared secure keychain.
